# Video 1: Sign In and Find Your Way Around

Audience: members with an approved, activated account.

Final video: 1 minute 20 seconds, including the closing frame.

## Narration

Hey everyone. Here's how to get into your Engineering Club account and find what you need.

Open the member sign-in page at the address on screen. Use the Oberlin email you signed up with and the password you chose when you activated your account. Then select Sign in.

Don't remember your password? Choose Forgot password. You can also use Sign in with an email link. Open the newest email and select Continue securely on the page it opens.

Once you're in, the dashboard is your starting point. Find a project shows you teams looking for members. Open a project that interests you and read what the team is working on.

When applications are open, tell the project lead why you'd like to join. Add any relevant skills, separated by commas, and select Send application. You can check its progress under My applications. Sending an application doesn't add you to the team immediately; the project lead reviews it first.

My teams is where your team workspace appears after you join. Check Invitations for team invites and Notifications for updates.

Have an idea of your own? Select Propose an idea on the dashboard. My ideas keeps track of your proposals.

Finally, My profile lets you update your interests and choose what other members can see. And when you're finished on a shared computer, sign out.
