# Video 2: The Officer Portal

Audience: invited officers. Keep this video separate from the public member guides.

Final video: 2 minutes 8 seconds, including the closing frame.

## Narration

Here's a quick tour of the officer portal. Open the admin address on screen and sign in with your officer account. A member account on its own doesn't give you admin access.

Start with Overview. Then use the sidebar, or Find a page, to go straight to the section you need.

Inbox contains messages and interest forms from the website. Mark reviewed means you've handled the message. It isn't the same as approving someone's membership. Leadership interest also doesn't automatically make someone an officer.

For membership decisions, open Member requests. Review the student's details before approving or rejecting the request.

Members shows the full account list. Needs approval is for requests waiting on a decision. Finishing setup is for people who still need to complete their account. Active shows members who can enter the portal.

To invite people yourself, select Add members. Paste their Oberlin email addresses, separated by commas or new lines. You can add up to twenty-five at a time. Review the list before sending, especially the spelling of each address.

An invitation records their membership approval, but they still need to verify their email and finish setup. If someone gets stuck, check their status and use Resend setup email. For an active member, use Send sign-in email. Tell them to open the newest message, and check Spam if it hasn't arrived.

Projects and Events are where you manage the club's published information. Open the item you want to edit, save your changes, and check its publishing status before expecting it to appear on the public site.

Project ideas is the review queue for member proposals. That's separate from editing an existing project.

Only a Super Admin can add officers. Under Settings, open Staff access, then Invite officer. Enter their email and name, choose Editor or Admin, and review the permissions before sending. Editors get access only to the sections you select.

That's the main flow: review requests, keep member setup moving, and keep the club's information current.
