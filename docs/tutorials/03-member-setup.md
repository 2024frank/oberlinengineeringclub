# Video 3: Set Up Your Member Account

Audience: first-time members, whether invited by an officer or requesting an account themselves.

Final video: 1 minute 39 seconds, including the closing frame.

## Narration

Let's get your Engineering Club account set up.

If an officer invited you, start with the welcome email sent to your Oberlin address. Open its setup link. On the page that opens, select Continue securely.

Your invitation already includes membership approval. After you verify your email, you can finish setting up your account without waiting for a second approval.

Choose a unique password with at least ten characters. Enter it again under Confirm password, then select Activate member account. Once activation finishes, you'll reach your member dashboard.

Haven't received an invitation? Open the member sign-in page at the address on screen and choose Request an account. Enter your full name and your Oberlin email address, then select Request account.

Check your email and complete the verification step. A club admin then reviews your request. Once it's approved, you'll receive a setup email so you can choose your password and activate the account.

If an email is missing, check Spam. Make sure it's from the club before opening the link. Always use the newest message. If a setup link has expired, ask an officer to resend it.

After signing in, open My profile. Add your class year, major, skills, and project interests. For fields marked Comma separated, put a comma between each item.

Scroll to Directory privacy. You decide whether to appear in the private member directory and which details other approved members can see. Your Oberlin email is private by default. Only turn on email sharing if you want other members to see it.

Select Save profile. Now you're ready to browse projects, meet teammates, or propose an idea of your own.
